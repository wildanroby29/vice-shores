**Vice Shores** — a sun-soaked 3D open-world crime game for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty. Rook, a new driver, runs 8 jobs for the fixer Marla across a neon coastal city: steal cars, outrun the VSPD, survive 5-star heat with a helicopter overhead, and become king of the shore.

- 3D low-poly city: beachfront boulevard, ocean, pier, marina, parks, market stalls
- Third-person on foot and behind the wheel, with carjacking and shooting
- Wanted 0–5 stars, police pursuit and ramming, helicopter spotlight, roadblocks
- 8 numbered story jobs, dense HUD (dispatch panel, minimap, respect ladder, crew, radio)
- Title screen with 3 save slots, garage upgrade shop, WebAudio synth sound
- Plays offline in desktop Chrome from static files — no network requests

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. WASD drive/walk, E enter & steal cars, Space shoot, Shift sprint, 1–4 weapons, Q/C/V crew abilities, G garage, P pause, M radio.

An original game: its city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound are all its own — nothing comes from Rockstar Games.
