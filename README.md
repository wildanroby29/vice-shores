**Vice Shores** is a 3D open-world crime game set in a bright, sun-soaked coastal city. Rook, a new driver in town, runs eight numbered jobs for the fixer Marla — stealing cars, running packages, outrunning the police — and climbs from NOBODY to BOSS of the shore.

## What is in the game

- **A living 3D city**: a street grid with colourful low-poly buildings and neon signs, a wide beachfront boulevard lined with palms, a bright blue ocean, a sandy beach, a pier, a marina, parks and a market — all in golden-hour light.
- **Third-person on foot and driving**: over-the-shoulder camera on foot, chase camera behind the car. Enter and exit cars with E; steal parked cars or carjack driven ones.
- **Traffic and pedestrians**: NPC cars loop the streets, 20+ pedestrians walk the sidewalks in small groups, flee crimes, and get knocked down.
- **Wanted 0–5 stars and the VSPD**: stars rise on theft, hitting pedestrians, ramming police and shooting. Police cars chase and ram with named dispatch units and tactics (RAMMING / FLANKING / PIT / ROADBLOCK / SPOTLIGHT); a helicopter with a spotlight follows at 4–5 stars; roadblocks appear at 3+ stars. Getting busted costs money and respawns Rook at the safehouse.
- **8 story jobs for Marla**: each with a name, act, payout ($250–$1,500), stages, map markers and dialogue — then free roam.
- **A dense HUD**: job panel, named police dispatch panel, wanted stars, money and clock, radio station and song, weapon slots with ammo, health and armour bars, minimap, respect/rank ladder, named crew abilities, dialogue box, and vehicle name, condition and speed.
- **Start and save**: a title screen with 3 rich save slots, pause, and localStorage saves (money, jobs, position).
- **Garage**: Dockside Customs sells repairs, resprays, armour, ammo and a nitro kit.
- **Sound**: WebAudio synth only — engine hum, two-tone siren, shots, crashes, pickups and mission jingles. No network requests, no external assets.

## How to play

Serve the game locally with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080 — or open `game/index.html` from a static server.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Drive / walk (W forward, A/D steer or turn) |
| E | Enter or exit a car / steal a car |
| Space / click | Shoot |
| Shift | Sneak on foot · nitro in a tuned car |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Fists / pistol / SMG |
| R | Refill ammo ($50) |
| Q / C / V | Crew: Mira swaps plates · Jai quick-fixes the car · Kiri hides you |
| M | Next radio station |
| C | Camera (in the title screen: menu) |
| G | Garage |
| P / Esc | Pause |

All names, places and art are original. No Rockstar material anywhere.
