# Vice Shores — a sun-soaked city of your own

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/wildanroby29/vice-shores/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/wildanroby29/vice-shores/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An original 3D open-world crime game for make.fun's **Make GTA 6** bounty. Rook is
new in town; the fixer Marla has eight jobs and a fast car. Drive the beachfront
boulevard, steal cars, outrun the VSPD, survive five wanted stars with a helicopter
overhead, and become the king of the shore.

**What's in it:** a sun-soaked coastal city (boulevard, beach, pier, marina, parks,
market, workshop) on foot and by car; traffic and 26 pedestrians; car theft; a
wanted system with police pursuit, ramming, roadblocks and a spotlight helicopter;
8 numbered story missions with payouts; combat (fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun); a
dense HUD with minimap, named police dispatch, respect ladder and crew abilities;
title screen with 3 save slots; a garage with repairs, respray, armour, ammo and
nitro; WebAudio synth sound; and a poster title screen.

**Play:** `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. WASD drive/walk, E enter
and steal cars, Space shoot, Shift sprint, 1–4 weapons, R reload, Q/C/V crew
abilities, G garage, P pause, M radio.

Built with three.js (vendored). Everything — city, characters, vehicles, names,
story, art and sound — is original; nothing comes from Rockstar Games.
