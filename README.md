A complete top-down open-world crime game for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty.
Drive and walk the neon coastal city of Vice Shores: steal cars, outrun the
police, dodge traffic, and run three story missions for the fixer Marla.

**Play:** open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or `node serve.ts` and
open http://localhost:8080). WASD/arrows to move and drive, Shift to sprint,
E to enter/exit or steal a car, Space or click to shoot, P to pause, M to mute.
